# Googleの地図サービスが変わった・終わった前例（2026-11-04 調査）

ことづての動画の冒頭（「明日、Googleマップの商用利用が禁止されたら？」）の根拠として調べた。4つの観点（日本、API と料金、サービス終了、規約）で探し、別の担当が1件ずつ反証を試みた。63件を確かめ、58件が確認または訂正つきで確認、5件は確認できなかった。生データは `private/raw/google-map-changes.json`（Git に入れない）。

## 書き方の注意

- ZENRIN wording: do not say Google 'stopped using', 'cut' or 'dropped' ZENRIN, and do not say '突然'. Google never confirmed it. On 2019-03-06 Google's blog announced a new map 'within the next few weeks', but it did not name ZENRIN. ZENRIN's president said on 2019-04-26 that business with Google was continuing. The confirmed facts are that the ZENRIN credit disappeared and roads and bus stops went missing. The press only inferred the cause.
- Do not show the ZENRIN share drop. The Bloomberg 13% figure was not fetched, and other sources differ (16%, limit-down, about 23% over two months).
- The current 2018 line '費用が数十倍になった例も' and the iStart source are not in the verified set, so I replaced them. If you want a number, INTERNET Watch 2018-05-31 gives the free amount falling from 25,000 per day to about 28,000 per month for Maps JavaScript API.
- I dropped the 2006 item, so the on-screen source line must change. New line: INTERNET Watch（2012年6月）、GIS Resources（2015年1月）、Google Open Source Blog（2017年2月）、INTERNET Watch（2018年6月）、週刊アスキー（2019年3月）、AUTOMATON（2021年10月）、INTERNET Watch（2025年2月）。2026年11月4日に確認.
- 2018 price rise (added 2026-11-07): the overage price went from USD 0.50 to USD 7 per 1,000 loads (GIGAZINE 2018-07-23 http://gigazine.net/news/20180723-farewell-google-maps/ , I Programmer 2018-07-16 https://www.i-programmer.info/news/145-mapping-a-gis/11978-google-maps-api-changes-billing-rules.html). Do not use '1,400%' (a developer-reported average in Geoawesomeness, not Google's figure) or '費用が数十倍'. Do not use 2025 as a price-rise example (some bills went up, others went to 0). Do not say '突然': most changes had notice (about 40 days for the 2018 key requirement, about a year for Earth API, Fusion Tables, Maps Engine and Earth Pro).
- The 2012 date is approximate. Charges were announced on 2011-10-27, sources disagree on when billing started, and Google said it would contact sites instead of billing them automatically. '有料化' describes the policy, not actual bills.
- The Maps Engine data removal comes from a blog quoting Google's email. Actual deletion was not confirmed independently, which is why the line says '消去へ'.
- Pacing: 7 items in 11 to 14 seconds is about 1.6 to 2.0 seconds each. The lines are 16 to 22 characters, so viewers will not read every line. Narration should carry the point, for example 'Googleは自分の判断で、何度も変えてきた'.
- For 2022 the year is the end of service (2022-12-31), but the source is the announcement article from 2021-10. If the year and the source month must match, use '2021' with the line '提供終了を発表。ドラクエウォークも別サービスへ'.
- Some cases failed verification and should not be used: Business Profile websites/chat dates (TechRadar), Places Radar Search, Tour Builder/Poly, the 'geocoding only on a Google map' clause, and Earth Engine commercial tiers. Also do not use 'Google apologized' for 2019, which is unverified.
